About LikhaLuntian.

Our name comes from two Filipino words: Likhaᜎᜒᜃ᜔ᜑ and Luntianᜎᜓᜈ᜔ᜆᜒᜀᜈ᜔. Together they describe something made, made green.

Shop Sustainably Read more about our story ↓
✓ Locally made100% Filipino

LikhaLuntian started as a small Batangas-based effort to connect local makers who were already working sustainably with shoppers looking for better everyday alternatives. What began as a handful of sellers has grown into a marketplace built entirely around one promise: nothing sold here costs the planet more than it gives back.

From Workshop to Your Doorstep

Every LikhaLuntian product starts with a Filipino maker and a natural, low-waste material. The video follows that journey so you can see who made your purchase and how.

What You'll See

  • Sourcing: plant-based and recycled materials such as bamboo, abaca, rattan and paper, gathered close to home.
  • Handcrafting: artisans weaving, shaping, pouring and assembling each piece by hand.
  • Quality checks: every maker is vetted by hand before their products reach the shelf.
  • Low-waste packing: orders are packed with minimal, reusable or recyclable materials.

Additional info about the video

Why Eco-Friendly Products Are Important for Business Growth

Shoppers increasingly want to buy from businesses that share their values. Eco-friendly products cut waste and emissions, build a trusted reputation, and can save money over time because they last longer. Switching to sustainable materials is good for the planet and good for growth.

Angelica Reyes
I switched my whole bathroom to LikhaLuntian's personal care line. Months later, the bamboo toothbrush and wooden comb still look brand new, and I love that every peso goes straight to a small maker. Angelica Reyes · Customer · Makati City

Before LikhaLuntian, our vermicompost only sold within Cavite. Now orders come from all over the country, and I've been able to hire two more people from our barangay to help on the farm. Ramon Bautista · Farmer · Cavite Vermi Farms
