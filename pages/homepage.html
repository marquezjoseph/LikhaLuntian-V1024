Voices from Our Community

Real stories from the shoppers and makers who make LikhaLuntian possible. Hover or tap a photo to read their story.

Angelica Reyes
I switched my whole bathroom to LikhaLuntian's personal care line. Months later, the bamboo toothbrush and wooden comb still look brand new, and I love that every peso goes straight to a small maker. Angelica Reyes · Customer · Makati City
Ramon Bautista
Before LikhaLuntian, our vermicompost only sold within Cavite. Now orders come from all over the country, and I've been able to hire two more people from our barangay to help on the farm. Ramon Bautista · Farmer · Cavite Vermi Farms
Marites Villanueva
Weaving abaca bags used to mean selling only to neighbors and the town market. Through LikhaLuntian, our cooperative now ships our bags to shoppers we'll probably never meet, and that keeps our tradition alive. Marites Villanueva · Weaver · Bicol Weaving Cooperative
Jericho Salazar
I was skeptical about ordering handmade goods online, but the tote bag and pencil case I got were sturdier than anything I've bought in a mall. Knowing the maker's name on the listing makes it feel personal. Jericho Salazar · Customer · Batangas City
